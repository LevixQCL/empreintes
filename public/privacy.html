OSSYRA

Privacy and data

Updated November 9, 2026 · Version française

In short

  • The game never asks for your name, address or date of birth. Other players only see your username.
  • Your game is saved on your device and, if you are 13 or older, in your online account so you never lose it.
  • No ads, no trackers, nothing is sold or shared for commercial purposes. Only reCAPTCHA (Google) checks that requests really come from the game and not from a bot.
  • You can delete everything yourself, at any time, from within the game.

Who is responsible

Ossyra (French title: EMPREINTES) is an independent game in alpha, created and published by Bryan Houben (Belgium), the controller of your data.

Contact for anything about your data: ossyragame@gmail.com. Most requests can also be handled directly in the game (see “Your rights” below).

What is stored, and why

Your gameSo you can get it back (on this device, and in your account if you have one). This is the performance of the game you request.
Your accountA technical identifier created automatically (“invisible” account), or your Google account or email if you choose to protect your game. Your email is only used to sign in: it is never shown to other players.
What other players seeYour username, your depth, your Grand Board scores, the Bearer leading your squad, your garrison (raids), your guard on the Spire and, at the Quayside Circle, your showcase, your duplicates and your card wishes. You can withdraw from the Grand Board in the game.
Quayside CircleYour friendships, trade offers and duel challenges, visible only to you and the director involved.
ReferralYour referrer’s code and the milestones reached, to reward you both.
Settings and incidentsSound, animations, graphics and a small error log stay on your device only.

Where your data is

The account and online save use Firebase (Google), whose servers may be located in the United States. This transfer is covered by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Google’s contractual clauses.

Anti-cheat protection (reCAPTCHA): to check that requests really come from the game and not from an automated program, the game uses Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA (Google). On the game’s website, reCAPTCHA analyses technical information about your device and browser (for example IP address, browser type, page movements) and sends it to Google. This information is only used to detect bots and abuse; it is not used to show you ads. This is our legitimate interest in protecting the game and its leaderboards. Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub), which, like any host, sees visitors’ IP addresses in order to serve the pages. Fonts and images are served by the game’s own site, with no calls to other services.

How long

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, everything it published is erased immediately.

Minimum age

In Belgium, you can consent on your own to the processing of your data online from the age of 13. On first launch, the game asks whether you are 13 or older. If not, you play the full game, but nothing goes online: no account, no leaderboard, no Circle, your game stays on your device.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or erase your data, and object to its processing:

  • Delete everything: in the game, Menu › Account › “Delete my account and data”. Your game, username, Grand Board entry, garrison, Spire guard, showcase and Circle friends are erased, then the account itself.
  • Without access to the game (lost device, app uninstalled): write to ossyragame@gmail.com with your username, from the email linked to your account if you have one. Everything is erased within 30 days at most, and you receive a confirmation.
  • Change your username or withdraw from the Grand Board: in the game, at any time.
  • For anything else, write to ossyragame@gmail.com: reply within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority: dataprotectionauthority.be.
